## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Conversion, Our intention is to give indebted people a fair way forward, and a plan they can’t keep isn’t one. Today consumers pick plans that look right but are too high, and missed payments rise.
- **Target audience:** Financially stretched consumers

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, The change is in-product and reaches about 4,630 consumers a month at plan review, so there is no external audience to market to

## Channels

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** PM: owns the plan, the A/B test, the ship/iterate decision and post-launch metrics. 
Collections team lead: owns the agent briefing and one-pager. 
client account manager: owns the client notification
CRM/marketing: owns the email and the demo
Budget: no extra cost beyond team time, because the change is in-product.
- **Timeline:** Phase 1, beta (weeks 1 to 2, then the test): compliance sign-off, agent and client briefing, QA boundary tests, then a 50/50 A/B test for about 4 months (about 2 months of enrollment plus 60 days for outcomes) with no external comms. Phase 2, launch moment (the week after the ship decision): roll out to 100%, send the email, hand agents the one-pager and send the client note. Phase 3, post-launch (weeks 1 to 8 after launch): track plans kept and the agreement-rate guardrail weekly, review complaints, and make the iterate call at week 8 using the ship/iterate rule

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** Plans kept per journey starter (primary): 37.3% to 38.8% (+1.5 points), measured over the 60 days after agreement.
Missed-payment rate within 60 days: from 19.0% toward 15.6%.
Agreement rate (guardrail): stays within 1.8 points of control, floor 44.2%.
- **Bad signal to watch for:** Agreement rate falls more than 1.8 points below control while missed payments barely move, which means the labels are scaring people off without making plans more sustainable.
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Iterate. If the lift is significant but under +1.5 points, keep the check and tune the thresholds; ship fully if it reaches +1.5 with the guardrail intact.
