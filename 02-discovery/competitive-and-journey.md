# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** A financially stretched consumer with one or more open claims who is anxious about making a mistake.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Commit to a payment plan they can actually sustain so the debt is resolved.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** Faced with a list of plans and no affordability guidance, they picked the shortest term because it “looked like the right answer.” The installment was too high and they missed the second payment. Underneath is fear (“What if they use it against me?”) that makes them guess rather than ask.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** None
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** - Opens the plan list with no guidance on what they can afford.
- Scans the options and applies a rule of thumb: the plan with the lowest number of months “looked like the right answer.”
- Commits to that plan.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** Pays the first installment. Misses the second payment because the amount was too high.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** the plan with the lowest number of months “looked like the right answer.”
