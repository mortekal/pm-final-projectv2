# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which Riverty business-unit case are you using?:** A fair way forward
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** Clients expect digital self-service from collection partners. If we do nothing for 12 months, competitors that offer it will win tenders and beat us on recovery and cost-to-collect.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** A debtor who can’t pay in full searches online and budgets in a spreadsheet, then calls, waits on hold, and gives up. Some ignore the letters while the balance grows
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** Debtors willing to pay in installments can’t set up a plan without calling an agent, so many default, and clients are moving to partners with fair self-service options.
- **Value proposition, based on the initiative in your assigned Riverty case, what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** Debtors set up a fair installment plan themselves, any time, without calling an agent. That means more plans kept, less default, lower cost-to-collect, and the experience clients now expect. It’s urgent because every month without it risks lost contracts.
